# News Intelligenz

News Intelligenz helps immigrants living in Germany understand parliamentary activity through English explanations grounded in official records.

## Language

**Reader**:
An immigrant living in Germany who wants to follow parliamentary activity, particularly someone who does not speak German as a first language.

**Explainer**:
An English explanation of a parliamentary proceeding grounded in its documented steps and source documents.

**Parliamentary activity**:
Parliamentary proceedings and their documented steps in the selected DIP electoral terms, including Bundestag, Bundesrat, Bundesversammlung, and Europakammer activity.
_Avoid_: Bundestag updates (when referring to the full scope)

**Parliamentary proceeding (Vorgang)**:
A parliamentary matter followed across its individual steps, which may have an overall summary and state of deliberation. It is distinct from the documents about it.

**Proceeding step (Vorgangsposition)**:
An individual documented step within a parliamentary proceeding, attributed to a parliamentary institution and providing evidence of the proceeding's development.
_Avoid_: Update (a changed record alone may be a correction)

**Parliamentary document reference**:
A reference from a proceeding step to an official document, such as a Drucksache or Plenarprotokoll, with available document identifiers and links.

**Parliamentary record version**:
A retained state of a proceeding or proceeding step as observed during collection. A changed record may represent a correction or added description rather than a new parliamentary development.

**Discovery**:
An encounter with a parliamentary proceeding or proceeding step through a DIP source during a collection run. Multiple discoveries can refer to the same underlying record.

**Semantic retrieval**:
Finding source records by meaning, including across German and English queries, and returning the source material that supports the match.
