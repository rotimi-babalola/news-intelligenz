# News Intelligenz

News Intelligenz helps immigrants living in Germany understand news about the country and government activity through English explanations.

## Language

**Reader**:
An immigrant living in Germany who wants to follow the country's news and government activity, particularly someone who does not speak German as a first language.

**German news**:
Reporting about politics and everyday life in Germany, including areas such as healthcare. It is not limited to immigration news or public publishers; configured public-news feeds may also contribute broader reporting.
_Avoid_: German-language news (language alone does not define the scope)

**Explainer**:
An English explanation that makes source reporting understandable to a reader.

**Bundestag update**:
Information published by the German federal parliament and made available through its RSS feeds.

**State parliament**:
The parliament of one of Germany's 16 federal states (Bundesländer).

**Publication**:
A distinct article or institutional document issued by a publisher. Separate publishers' reports about the same event are separate publications.

**Publication version**:
A retained state of a publication's text. A correction or text update belongs to the same publication while preserving its earlier text.

**Possible duplicate**:
Two records suspected of representing the same publication, without sufficient identity evidence to merge automatically. Similar headlines alone do not establish that they are the same publication.

**Discovery**:
An encounter with a publication through a particular feed or search query during a collection run. Multiple discoveries can refer to the same publication.

**Relevance review**:
A manual decision about whether a search candidate with uncertain relevance belongs in the collection. Items from configured RSS feeds do not require relevance review.
